This is a guide to becoming a printer for [#printing-legion][slack]!

Signing up as a printer is quite easy, there's only a handful of requirements! You need to:

- Own a 3D printer, or have unrestricted access to one! That is, no library, school or shared club printers!
- Be able to package and send out packages.
- Not be banned from Hackatime for fraud.
- Be 18 or younger.

To sign up, you need to fill this form → https://forms.hackclub.com/printing-legion

Once you sign up, you may also need a grant to cover shipping costs. To receive a $10 shipping grant, fill out this form → https://forms.hackclub.com/printing-legion-active-grants

## Claiming a Print!

You can claim any print in [#printing-legion][slack] that's close to you.

- Shipping must be less than $10 (but exceptions are granted if needed!).
- Make sure that you communicate with the recipient on details such as shipping cost, slicer settings, filament types, etc. Note that only PLA and PETG (translucent, matte, or silk variants included) are allowed. If a person requires TPU, ABS, ASA or another engineering material for their print, please run it by me ([@nimit][nimit])
- Printing is not free, and you need to tell the recipient exactly how much the print costs! Costs are fixed at $50/kg (whatever it says in the slicer) plus any additional shipping costs. For example, a 200 gram print that needs $8 to ship will cost $18. You need to tell your recipient to keep this amount of money in their grant unused, as it will be withdrawn after shipping.
- ⚠️ You are not allowed to exchange sensitive card information such as card numbers or the CVV!
- Once you print their parts, use your shipping grant to book a parcel or purchase a label! You'll need to ask the person for two main details: their shipping address and their HCB card URL. The card URL can be found in the address bar on the page for their HCB card, and looks something like this → `https://hcb.hackclub.com/grants/......`
- After shipping the package, you need to fill out this form → https://forms.hackclub.com/sent-print

## Prizes and Filament

You can claim one spool of filament for every 750g of filament you print! You can use this towards any PLA or PETG spool (translucent, matte and silk included) but not for CF or GF (Glass Fiber) engineering variants.

Being a part of Print Legion also earns you certain rewards.

- After 3 prints shipped, you can get a $25 grant to buy packing materials like boxes, tape, bubble wrap, mailers, labels, and more! This list is non-exhaustive, feel free to run stuff by me if you want it! You can get another $25 grant after every 9 prints.
- After 6 prints shipped, you can get a $30 grant to buy stickers to send out with your prints or give away at events! We'd appreciate if these stickers were custom designs :)
- After 12 prints shipped, you can get a $60 grant to buy a label printer to make sending out packages much easier!

If you think you qualify for any of these extra prizes, DM me to request your grant!\
Grants are sent to the email address you used when signing up for Print Legion, you can ask me to get it changed to your preferred HCB email.\
A print shipped is considered one project!

## FAQ

### I want to sent multiple projects in one package to save on shipping costs, am I allowed to do this?

Yes! Make sure to divide the shipping cost between form submissions, and submit once for each project. For example, if shipping costs $9 and I ship 3 projects to the same person in the same box, then I need to submit the form thrice with $3 as the shipping cost.

### Someone wants me to print ABS for their hackpad because it's stronger.

For projects that don't need the strength, only PLA and PETG are allowed!

### Am I allowed to take multiple print jobs simultaneously?

Please only take one print task at a time! This makes sure that everyone gets a chance to participate. If you do have multiple tasks because of certain circumstances, make sure to prioritize them properly and communicate status updates.

### I printed the parts but the recipient ghosted me :c

Please DM [@nimit][nimit] to get this resolved! Your prints will still count towards your next spool and prizes.
