## FAQ:

**Can I submit personal prints?** \
Nope! Printing Legion is only for Hack Club [YSWS](https://ysws.hackclub.com) programs!

**How does filament reimbursement work?** \
For every 750g of filament you print and ship, you get a $20 HCB card grant to get 1 new spool of filament!

**What other rewards do I get?** \
Glad you asked! Apart from filament reimbursement, you can also unlock rewards based on how many print requests you send out:

- 3 print jobs: a $25 Packing material grant! (this one refreshed every 9 print jobs)
- 6 print jobs: a $30 sticker grant to get stickers from Jukebox!
- 12 print jobs: a $60 grant to get a label printer! This makes life 10x easier

Note that these are counted _per project_, not by number of prints (i.e a hackpad with 3 parts still counts as 1 print!)

**How long has this been running?** \
Since December 2024!
